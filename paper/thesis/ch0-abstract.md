# 摘要

洗钱交易监测是金融机构的法定义务，也是监管科技领域的典型难题：洗钱样本占比约千分之一且时序分布漂移，模型输出的监管可解释性是进入合规流程的硬约束，而文献中普遍存在的评估泄漏使跨方法比较失去意义。现有研究或以检测精度为唯一目标而忽视归因，或依赖端到端图神经网络而无法回答"为何标记此笔交易"，且特征级归因在高度冗余的特征集上因特征可互换性而系统性失效。

针对上述问题，本文提出因果行为画像与组级反事实归因框架（CBP-GCA）。在检测侧，以严格时间可见性约束将交易图的边结构、实体结构与对手方邻域压缩为三层递进的 78 列因果行为特征——账户因果窗口、账户对交互与实体差分信号、对手方快照画像——注入正则化 XGBoost 进行风险排序，每层特征增量单独可测且全部具备反洗钱业务语义。在归因侧，将特征组织为对应洗钱放置、分层、整合阶段的 8 个行为族，干预语义锚定为"置回普通账户水平"，反事实直接作用于检测器本体而非代理模型，为每个被标记案例自动生成与可疑活动报告叙事同构的反事实瀑布解释。在评估侧，确立无泄漏时序协议，并以泄漏法医实验量化协议缺陷的影响；进一步构造特征数递减的检测器族，首次测得反洗钱任务上精度与归因敏感度的帕累托前沿。

在 507 万笔交易的公开数据集上的时序外推实验表明：本文框架平均精度（AUPRC）达 0.6214，为随机基线的 345 倍，ROC-AUC 0.9904；仅审核风险排序前 0.1% 的交易即可捕获 47.1% 的洗钱行为且告警精度 83.4%；组级反事实显示账户对高频互转与实体内跨账户差分两个行为族解释了标记成因的绝大部分（干预降幅 0.93/0.88），遮蔽检验确认归因有效性；精度-可归因性前沿显示 30 列稀疏部署可取得最高归因敏感度（0.968），为不同监管强度的机构提供菜单式选择。系统性诊断实验进一步表明，端到端图神经网络（GraphSAGE、GATv2 及其增强形态）与 GraphMAE 式自监督图表示在无泄漏协议下均不可复现文献声称的优势，而将图神经网络的输入特征以因果聚合形态直接拼接给梯度提升树可提升平均精度 0.098——交易图结构信息的有效载体是显式因果特征而非端到端嵌入。全部结果满足 5 种子可复现协议，跨环境验证一致。

**关键词**：反洗钱；交易风险监测；因果特征；反事实归因；可解释机器学习；梯度提升树

# Abstract

Anti-money laundering (AML) transaction monitoring is a statutory obligation of financial institutions and a representative challenge of regulatory technology: positive samples account for roughly one in a thousand with temporal distribution drift, regulatory explainability is a hard constraint for compliance adoption, and pervasive evaluation leakage in the literature invalidates cross-method comparison. Existing work either pursues detection accuracy alone while neglecting attribution, or relies on end-to-end graph neural networks (GNNs) that cannot answer "why was this transaction flagged"; feature-level attribution systematically fails on highly redundant feature sets due to feature interchangeability.

This thesis proposes Causal Behavioural Profiling with Group-Counterfactual Attribution (CBP-GCA). On the detection side, edge, entity, and counterparty structures of the transaction graph are compressed under strict temporal visibility constraints into 78 causal behavioural features in three progressive layers — account causal windows, account-pair interaction and entity-differential signals, and counterparty snapshot profiles — fed into a regularized XGBoost risk ranker, with each layer's increment measured in isolation. On the attribution side, features are organized into 8 behaviour groups corresponding to the placement, layering, and integration stages of money laundering; the intervention semantics are anchored at "restore to ordinary-account level", and counterfactuals act directly on the detector itself rather than a surrogate, automatically generating a counterfactual waterfall explanation isomorphic to Suspicious Activity Report narratives for every flagged case. On the evaluation side, a leakage-free temporal protocol is established, its violation is quantified by leakage forensics, and a family of increasingly sparse detectors yields the first measured accuracy–attributability Pareto frontier for AML.

Temporal extrapolation experiments on a public dataset of 5.07 million transactions show that the framework achieves AUPRC 0.6214 (345× the random baseline) with ROC-AUC 0.9904; reviewing only the top-0.1% risk-ranked transactions captures 47.1% of laundering with 83.4% alert precision. Group-level counterfactuals reveal that two behaviour groups — account-pair high-frequency transfers and intra-entity cross-account differentials — explain nearly the entire flagging cause (drops of 0.93/0.88), with fidelity checks confirming attribution validity; the frontier shows that a 30-feature sparse deployment attains the highest attribution sensitivity (0.968). Systematic diagnostics further demonstrate that end-to-end GNNs (GraphSAGE, GATv2, and enhanced variants) and GraphMAE-style self-supervised graph representations fail to reproduce claimed advantages under the leakage-free protocol, whereas feeding the GNN input features as causal aggregates to gradient-boosted trees improves AUPRC by 0.098 — the effective carrier of transaction-graph structural information is explicit causal features, not end-to-end embeddings. All results satisfy a 5-seed reproducible protocol with cross-environment consistency.

**Key words**: anti-money laundering; transaction risk monitoring; causal features; counterfactual attribution; explainable machine learning; gradient-boosted trees
